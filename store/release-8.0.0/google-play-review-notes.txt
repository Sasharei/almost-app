Almost Savings requires no account or sign-in. Version 8.0.0 redesigns navigation, onboarding, goals, budget, reports, savings decisions and widgets.

Automatic expenses: Profile > Settings > Automatic expenses. With Almost Premium, choose bank/payment apps, enable import and grant notification access in Android Settings. Android grants broad notification access; Almost processes only future individual notifications from the apps chosen by the user. It excludes group summaries and existing notifications. Supported positive purchase amounts with explicit currencies are recorded locally; ambiguous messages are retained for review. Capture depends on notification content and platform/vendor restrictions. No bank credentials are requested. Users can edit/delete records and pause import. Existing records remain editable without Premium. Financial notification data is not sent to advertising networks.

Shopping Pause and Apple Pay integration are iOS features and are not advertised as Android capabilities. Savings are user-recorded decisions, not verified bank balances. Optional Premium purchases use Google Play Billing; restore/manage routes remain available. No advertising placements are displayed.

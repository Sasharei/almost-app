# ASO direction for 7.0.1

## Positioning

Almost should own the moment before checkout rather than look like another expense ledger. The core promise is: pause the impulse, see its monthly effect, and turn the money you keep into visible progress.

The strongest differentiated bundle in the current product is:

1. temptation cards and Think Later for the decision moment;
2. adaptive limits, weekly reports, and the 30-day impulse map for understanding patterns;
3. goals, streaks, challenges, rewards, and Almi for sustained motivation;
4. subscription visibility and widgets for everyday context.

The copy deliberately avoids implying banking, payment execution, or provider-confirmed transfers. Almost is positioned as a decision and planning tool.

## Search intent clusters

- Primary: stop impulse buying, impulse spending, achats impulsifs, compras impulsivas, Impulskäufe, покупки импульсивно.
- Secondary: savings tracker, no-spend challenge, saving goals, money habits, budget planner.
- Feature: subscription tracker, weekly spending report, spending triggers, impulse map.

Apple keywords do not repeat the brand name or title phrases unnecessarily. Google descriptions use the same intent terms naturally instead of keyword blocks.

## Visual narrative

1. Premium is active — the refreshed 7.0.1 world and Almi.
2. Plan the month — adaptive category limits.
3. See the pattern — weekly and monthly reports.
4. Pause the impulse — triggers and impulse map.
5. More room for plans — goals, categories, and history.
6. Everything is ready — a calm, complete money routine.

The Google feature graphic keeps Almi and the planning cards inside the central safe area and contains no text, price claims, rankings, store badges, or device imagery.

## First experiments

- Product page A: lead with impulse control (`01-welcome`, `04-impulse`, `03-insights`).
- Product page B: lead with practical money planning (`02-budget`, `03-insights`, `05-unlimited`).
- Product page C: lead with motivation and Almi (`01-welcome`, `06-ready`, `02-budget`).

Measure product-page conversion by locale before changing the whole set. Keep the screenshots, title, and short description aligned so each experiment communicates one clear promise.

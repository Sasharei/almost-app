# Almost onboarding illustration prompt set

Generated for the onboarding redesign approval package. All outputs are project-bound and live in `design/onboarding-redesign/assets/`.

## Shared art direction

> A premium tactile soft-3D product illustration for the Almost mindful-spending mobile app. Calm, friendly, collectible-diorama quality; rounded clay-like forms, matte materials, soft studio lighting, restrained violet, mint, warm peach, sky blue, and gold palette. Clean silhouette, readable at mobile size, no text, no letters, no brand marks, no watermark, no UI screenshot, transparent background. Match the visual lineage of Almost's existing Almi mascot and premium benefit scenes without copying their composition.

## Hero illustrations

- `hero-language.png`: Almi beside a small friendly globe, two blank speech-bubble forms, and a few gold/lilac currency tokens; welcoming setup scene with generous negative space.
- `hero-privacy.png`: Almi beside a translucent protective shield surrounding a small abstract analytics chart; privacy, control, and trust rather than surveillance.
- `hero-goals.png`: Almi gently rolling a gold coin along a curved violet path toward a clear savings jar, with a few small goal tokens; visible progress and purposeful saving.

Hero output: 3:2 RGBA PNG, transparent background, no checkerboard baked into the pixels.

## Persona mini illustrations

Each asset uses the shared direction and this additional instruction:

> One isolated compact vignette only, centered, square canvas, bold readable silhouette at 72–96 px, 3–5 objects maximum, transparent background, no mascot, no person, no text, no letters, no logos, no watermark.

- `persona-coffee.png`: ceramic coffee cup, steam curl, two coffee beans, small gold coin.
- `persona-smoking.png`: cleanly broken cigarette, tiny green sprout, subtle fresh-air arc; positive and non-graphic.
- `persona-beauty.png`: lipstick, compact mirror, small cosmetics bottle, one sparkle.
- `persona-gamer.png`: modern game controller, two tiny reward tokens, subtle energy arcs; no platform branding.
- `persona-delivery.png`: warm takeout box, pizza slice, small receipt roll with no writing.
- `persona-online.png`: parcel box, smartphone with blank screen, shopping bag, small cursor shape.
- `persona-home.png`: small table lamp, rounded vase with leaf, folded cushion.
- `persona-anime.png`: generic manga book with blank cover, stylized cat figurine, small starburst; no copyrighted character.
- `persona-subscriptions.png`: stack of generic service tiles with abstract play/music/cloud symbols and a small calendar tile with no writing.
- `persona-fashion.png`: clean sneaker, small handbag, folded garment, one soft sparkle; no fashion logo.

Persona output: square 1254 × 1254 RGBA PNG with transparent background.

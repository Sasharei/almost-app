1. **Stance:** calm spending control, honest product benefits and store offers, no invented urgency or social proof.
2. **World:** established Almost cat on blue clouds and hills; bold dark/indigo headline; themed rounded content surface and outline icons.
3. **Topology:** hero → three short benefits → all-benefits disclosure/trial switch → cancellation reassurance → parallel monthly/yearly/weekly choices → single pinned purchase button, complete small billing text and restoration/legal links. Expanded details retain every original insight route. Management remains reachable by scrolling.
4. **Rhythm:** ordinary-phone hero208, benefit48, cards174, content inset20, CTA50; short normal-text screens use a compact88-point hero and140-point cards; large-text screens stack cards; tablets use a176-point hero and bound the sheet to850 high and620 wide.
5. **Signature:** exact eligible trial duration on a green ribbon attached to the yearly card, native total annual price visible beside monthly equivalent. Selected radio mark provides a non-color cue.
